Hackathons

My first Hackathon was Digi Challenge for Change organised by Digi in 2013, I won RM25,000 by Marena, an app that helps users locate and arrange sport matches with other teams.

For the next two years, I won runner up in D3 App Development Contest in 2014, and first place for Big Data Analytics Challenge in Angelhack in 2015.

In 2017, I ended my Hackathon journey with Hack Horizon's First Prize in London, Hack Horizon is a 80-hour Hackathon which took off from Hong Kong and landed at London. The action took place at 30,000 feet on a public flight with 32 of the best entrepreneurs, engineers and designers racing to build products and services to boost travellers’ experience.
